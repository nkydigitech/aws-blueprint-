What is AWS?

Amazon Web Services is the world's most comprehensive and broadly adopted cloud platform, offering over 200 fully featured services from data centers globally.

☁️

Cloud Computing

AWS provides on-demand delivery of IT resources over the internet with pay-as-you-go pricing. No upfront costs, no long-term contracts.

🌎

Global Infrastructure

33 geographic regions, 105 Availability Zones, and 450+ Edge Locations. Deploy your applications closer to your users for lower latency.

🔒

Security First

AWS is architected to be the most secure cloud computing environment. Used by banks, governments, and military organizations worldwide.

⚡

Infinite Scale

Scale from a single server to thousands in minutes. Handle millions of requests without breaking a sweat or changing your architecture.

💰

Cost Effective

Pay only for what you use. AWS Free Tier lets you explore 100+ services free for 12 months, with some services always free.

🔧

200+ Services

From compute, storage, and databases to machine learning, IoT, and quantum computing - everything you need to build anything.

🎯 Why Learn AWS in 2026?

AWS holds 32% of the global cloud market share. The average AWS Solutions Architect salary exceeds $150,000/year. Over 1 million businesses run on AWS. Learning AWS isn't just adding a skill - it's future-proofing your career.

ℹ️
AWS Free Tier

New AWS accounts get 12 months of Free Tier access, including 750 hours/month of EC2 t2.micro, 5GB of S3 storage, and 1 million Lambda requests per month. Perfect for learning without spending a dime.
